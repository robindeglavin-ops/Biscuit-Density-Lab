Purpose and Introduction

Purpose: To evaluate density of irregular objects using the concepts of mass and volume.

When baking, the density of an ingredient can affect how much of it is needed to achieve the desired result. In this lab, you will practice determining density by measuring the mass and volume of biscuit ingredients. You will then use your measurements to calculate density and compare the densities of different ingredients. Density describes how much mass is contained within a given volume, so accurate measurements matter.
